# Sign and normalization sheet

- Metric: \(g_{\mu\nu}=\operatorname{diag}(+1,-1,-1,-1)\).
- \(\gamma_5=i\gamma^0\gamma^1\gamma^2\gamma^3\), \(\gamma_5^2=1\).
- \(\sigma_{\mu\nu}=i[\gamma_\mu,\gamma_\nu]/2\).
- \(\epsilon^{0123}=+1\), \(\epsilon_{0123}=-1\).
- Fourier transform: \(f(x)=\int d^4k\,(2\pi)^{-4}e^{-ikx}\widetilde f(k)\).
- \(p'=p+q\), where \(p'\) is the initial \(1^+\) momentum, \(p\) the final
  \(1^-\) momentum, and \(q^2=0\).
- \(e>0\); \(Q_s=-1/3\), \(Q_c=+2/3\), \(Q_b=-1/3\).
- Borel rule:
  \(\mathcal B_{P^2\to M^2}[(s-P^2)^{-n}]
  =e^{-s/M^2}/[(n-1)!(M^2)^{n-1}]\).
- Equal double-Borel convention:
  \(M_1^2=M_2^2=2M^2\), \(u_0=1/2\), and
  \(\mathcal M^2=M^2\).
- Initial basis currents:
  \(J_A^\mu=\bar s\gamma^\mu\gamma_5Q\) and
  \(J_B^\mu=i\bar s\sigma^{\mu\nu}p'_\nu\gamma_5Q/(m_Q+m_s)\).
- Final current:
  \(J_V^\mu=\bar s\gamma^\mu Q\).
- Residues:
  \(\langle0|J_i^\mu|H_i(p',\eta)\rangle=f_i m_i\eta^\mu\) and
  \(\langle0|J_V^\mu|V(p,\xi)\rangle=f_Vm_V\xi^\mu\).
- Physical rotation:
  \(J_L=\sin\theta J_A+\cos\theta J_B\),
  \(J_H=\cos\theta J_A-\sin\theta J_B\).
- Retained leading radiative amplitude:
  \[
  {\cal M}=ie\,g\,\epsilon_{\alpha\beta\rho\sigma}
  \eta^\alpha\xi^{*\beta}\varepsilon^{*\rho}q^\sigma .
  \]
- Width:
  \[
  \Gamma(1^+\to1^-\gamma)=
  {\alpha_{\rm em}g^2k_\gamma^3(m_i^2+m_f^2)\over
  3m_i^2m_f^2},\qquad
  k_\gamma={m_i^2-m_f^2\over2m_i}.
  \]
