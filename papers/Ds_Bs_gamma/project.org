* Decisions
** Central prescription for the approved future calculation
- Continuum shape: a=1; diagonal crossing sigma_0=s_eff equals the Table II s0.
- Domain ratio: eta=tilde_s_A/tilde_s_P=m_A^2/m_P^2.
  Intercepts: tilde_s_P=s_eff*(1+eta)/eta and tilde_s_A=s_eff*(1+eta).
  They are not the two-point continuum thresholds.
- Borel ratio, independently defined: r=M1^2/M2^2=m_A^2/m_P^2 centrally.
  M1 belongs to the axial channel; M2 to the pseudoscalar.
- For K=M_eff^2: M1^2=(1+r)K, M2^2=(1+r)K/r,
  u0=1/(1+r) in the manuscript convention. Translate each reference's labels.
- Fix the continuum domain while scanning r. In paired comparisons use
  identical inputs; document whether sampled masses update central eta/r.
- Published angles remain external: Ds 26.6 +/- 0.6 degrees,
  Bs 38.5 +/- 0.1 degrees. No automatic refit of the original 2025 study.
- The old tensor-current normalization 1/(m_Q+m_s) was present in the
  calculations and absent only in print. This is an established author fact.
- Photon matching procedure is agreed for investigation; no replacement
  normalization or finite-mass scheme has yet been selected or validated.
