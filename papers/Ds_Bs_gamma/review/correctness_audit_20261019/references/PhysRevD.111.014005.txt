                In this study, we calculate the mixing angles between the axial-vector mesons D1ðs1Þ − D01ðs1Þ and
              B1ðs1Þ − B01ðs1Þ using the QCD sum rules approach. Our results are θ1 ¼ 28.2  0.6°, θ2 ¼ 26.6  0.6°,
              θ3 ¼ 38.6  0.1°, and θ4 ¼ 38.5  0.1°. These values are in good agreement with the predictions of heavy
              quark effective theory, particularly for the mixing angle θ ¼ 35.3°, and are compatible with several existing
              results in the literature. The predicted mixing angles can be tested through the analysis of semileptonic
              decays such as Bc → B1 lν, Bc → B0s1 lν, Bs → Ds1 lν, and Bs → D0s1 lν, which can be investigated at
              experimental facilities such as LHCb and Belle II.
