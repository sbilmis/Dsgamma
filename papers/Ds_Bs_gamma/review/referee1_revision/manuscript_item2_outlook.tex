Ratios of radiative widths into pseudoscalar and vector final states are
experimentally more accessible than absolute widths, particularly for
bottom-strange mesons, and may provide complementary information on the
mixing angle. A separate study of the transitions into $D_s^*\gamma$ and
$B_s^*\gamma$ is in preparation and will also examine these ratios and
their mixing-angle dependence, including the $D_{s1}(2536)$ channel.
