\begin{quote}
\itshape
\textbf{Continuum subtraction, p.~10 and Eqs.~(32)--(35), (B2), (B6).}
The same replacement
$e^{-m_Q^2/M^2}\to e^{-m_Q^2/M^2}-e^{-s_0/M^2}$
is applied to every photon-DA term, including terms with different powers
of $M^2$. This is not established merely by specifying quark--hadron duality.
Please state the duality region in the two dispersion variables and derive
the subtraction, including any boundary terms. Ref.~[25] applies this
replacement to the leading-twist term while leaving the higher-twist terms
unsubtracted. Please explain the difference and quantify its effect on the
cancellation-sensitive channel.
\end{quote}
