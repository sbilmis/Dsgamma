\subsection*{Completion of the leading mixed-condensate contribution}

Let $m=m_Q$, $d=m_Q+m_s$, $K=M^2$, and
$C_5=\langle\bar s g_s\sigma\!\cdot\!G s\rangle$.
We use the currents
\begin{equation}
J_A^\mu=\bar s\gamma^\mu\gamma_5 Q,
\qquad
J_B^\mu=\frac{i}{d}\bar s\sigma^{\mu\nu}p_\nu\gamma_5 Q,
\end{equation}
and the coefficient of $-g^{\mu\nu}+p^\mu p^\nu/p^2$ as the spin-one
invariant. At tree level, the leading dimension-five contribution contains
both the light-bilocal $-x^2 C_5/192$ term and the heavy-propagator term with
one background gluon contracted with the local quark--gluon operator.
The latter contribution, absent from the existing implementation, is
\begin{equation}
\delta\widehat{\boldsymbol\Pi}_5
=C_5e^{-m^2/K}\left(1-\frac{m^2}{K}\right)
\begin{pmatrix}
0 & \dfrac{1}{12d}\\[4pt]
\dfrac{1}{12d} & \dfrac{m}{6d^2}
\end{pmatrix}.
\end{equation}
The complete leading coefficients are therefore
\begin{align}
\widehat\Pi_{AA}^{(5)}
&=-C_5e^{-m^2/K}\frac{m^3}{4K^2},\\
\widehat\Pi_{AB}^{(5)}=\widehat\Pi_{BA}^{(5)}
&=C_5e^{-m^2/K}\frac{4K^2+2Km^2-3m^4}{12dK^2},\\
\widehat\Pi_{BB}^{(5)}
&=C_5e^{-m^2/K}\frac{m(2K^2+4Km^2-3m^4)}{12d^2K^2}.
\end{align}
The Wilson coefficients are evaluated at vanishing light mass, while the
specified current normalization $d=m_Q+m_s$ is retained. Terms proportional
to $m_s C_5$ and radiative corrections are not included.

For $m_s=0$, and hence $d=m$, the difference becomes
\begin{equation}
\left(\widehat\Pi_{AA}-\widehat\Pi_{BB}\right)_{C_5}
=-C_5e^{-m^2/K}\left(\frac{m}{3K}+\frac{1}{6m}\right).
\end{equation}
The existing light-bilocal contribution alone gives
$-C_5e^{-m^2/K}m/(2K)$, whereas the earlier printed appendix, under the
leading-term label assignment documented in the audit, gives
$-C_5e^{-m^2/K}m/K$. Thus the difference is not resolved by selecting one
of these two coefficients; the second contribution must be included.

The additional term vanishes in the $AA$ entry, so the pure axial-current
check is unaffected. Explicit symbolic traces and independent covariant
Dirac/color contractions agree. Axial, vector, and pseudoscalar control
channels reproduce their standard leading mixed-condensate terms.
This calculation establishes the leading coefficient in the present
approximation; it does not identify which formula was used in the original
2025 numerical program.
