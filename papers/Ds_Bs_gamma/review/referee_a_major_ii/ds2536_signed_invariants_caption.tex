\caption{Signed OPE contributions to (a) $\widehat\Pi_A$,
(b) $\widehat\Pi_B$, and (c)
$\widehat\Pi_2=\cos\theta\,\widehat\Pi_A
-\sin\theta\,\widehat\Pi_B$ for $D_{s1}(2536)\to D_s\gamma$,
as functions of the effective Borel parameter $M^2$.
The mixing angle is fixed at $\theta=26.6^\circ$, with
$M_1^2=M_2^2=2M^2$ and the remaining QCD inputs fixed at
the reference values used in the Borel scan.
The curves correspond to $s_0=9.5\,\mathrm{GeV}^2$;
the shaded bands show the envelope from varying
$s_0$ over $[9,10]\,\mathrm{GeV}^2$ and are not statistical
uncertainty intervals. The twist-four contribution includes
the retained two-particle and gluonic and electromagnetic
three-particle terms. The axial-current twist-three contribution
vanishes at $u_0=1/2$ in the present implementation.
The black curves show the sums of all displayed contributions.}
